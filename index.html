Research perspective

Geometry.
Design.
Fabrication.

I am interested in connecting digital representations of the human body with the design of physical objects.

My eyewear research uses smartphone-based facial reconstruction and facial landmarks to guide lens positioning. The frame is then refined around those lens positions and manufactured as a physical prototype.

The work brings together computer vision, geometric modeling, and computational fabrication, with an emphasis on making the transition from a reconstructed face to a personalized object.

  • Personalized eyewear
  • 3D reconstruction
  • Geometric modeling
  • Computational fabrication
